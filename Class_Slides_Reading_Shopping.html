VOCABULARY
Key words · learn before you read
NOUN
an impulse buy
something you buy suddenly, without planning it.
CCQ: Did you plan it? ✗ · Did you need it? Usually ✗
NOUN · /ˈbɑːɡɪn/
a bargain
something you buy much cheaper than usual — a great deal.
CCQ: Expensive or cheap? Cheap · Good or bad? Good!
NOUN / VERB
a refund
your money back when you return something.
CCQ: Do you keep the item? No · Get money back? Yes
