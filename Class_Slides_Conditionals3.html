FORM · THIRD
The third conditional · unreal past
If + past perfect → would have + V3
If I had revised, I would have passed.
condition — had + V3
result — would have + V3
🧠 100% past & imaginary — it didn't happen. Also: could have / might have + V3 for possibility.
Core 5 · THE SUMMIT · Third & Mixed Conditionals03 / 10
FORM · MIXED
The mixed conditional · past → now
Past cause → present result
If I had taken that job, I would be rich now.
condition — had + V3 (past)
result — would + verb (now)
🔑 The only change from the third conditional: drop have in the result when you mean now. Look for now, today, still.

TAKEAWAY
The rules to keep!
Conditionals — in a nutshell 🥜
Third: If + past perfect, would have + V3. 100% past & unreal.
Mixed: past condition → present result (would + verb, now).
Never would in the if-clause — use had + V3.
