Part 5 — Multiple choice 🎯
Read for detail, opinion and inference. Each answer is a paraphrase of the text — find it.
📖 The Four-Day Week
When a New Zealand firm trialled a four-day working week in 2018, few expected the results that followed. Rather than productivity collapsing, output remained stable — and in some teams it actually rose. Employees, granted an extra day of rest, reported sharper focus and lower stress, while the company saw absenteeism fall. Critics were quick to point out that the model may not translate to every sector; a hospital or a call centre cannot simply close its doors on a Friday. Yet the trial challenged a long-held assumption: that hours worked and value produced are one and the same. For knowledge workers in particular, the evidence increasingly suggests that well-rested minds accomplish more in less time — provided the goals are clear and distractions are kept to a minimum.
