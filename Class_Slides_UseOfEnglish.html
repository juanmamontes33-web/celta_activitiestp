PART 1
Multiple-choice cloze · 8 gaps
Part 1 — pick the right word 🎯
Four options, one gap. It tests collocation, phrasal verbs, linkers and fixed phrases — the words just "go together".
"You must pay attention." (not do / make / give attention)
💡 Read the whole sentence first — the answer often depends on the word after the gap.
Core 5 · THE SUMMIT · Use of English03 / 10
PART 2
Open cloze · one word, no options
Part 2 — ONE word per gap 🔑
No options given. Answers are almost always grammar words: articles (a/the), prepositions (at/in/on), auxiliaries (had/was), relatives (who/which), quantifiers.
"I'm not used to getting up early." · "The more you practise, the better you get."
💡 Never leave a gap empty, and never write two words — exactly one.
Core 5 · THE SUMMIT · Use of English04 / 10
PART 3
Word formation · change the root
Part 3 — build the right word 🔨
You're given a ROOT word in capitals. Change it to fit: add a prefix/suffix, and decide the part of speech.
IMPRESS → "an impressive result" · HELP → "very helpful" · GROW → "strong growth"
💡 Ask: noun, verb, adjective or adverb? And is it positive or negative (un-, in-, dis-)? Watch singular/plural too.
Core 5 · THE SUMMIT · Use of English05 / 10
PART 4
Key word transformations · the big one
Part 4 — same meaning, new words ⭐
1st sentence:
"Someone stole my bike."
KEY WORD: STOLEN →
"My bike was stolen last week."
⚠️ Rules: use 2–5 words · don't change the key word · contractions (don't) count as two words.

EXAM STRATEGY
Score more marks · habits
Four habits that win marks 🏆
Read the whole sentence before you fill a gap — context decides the answer.
Count your words in Part 4 (2–5). Too many or too few = 0 marks.
Never leave blanks — there's no penalty for a wrong guess.
Check spelling — a misspelled word in Parts 2–4 scores nothing.
