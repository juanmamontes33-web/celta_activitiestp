C: Yes, it's Daniela Ortiz — O-R-T-I-Z.
R: And the course runs on Thursday evenings, 6.30 to 8.
C: How much is it?
R: It's forty-five pounds for the term, with a ten-pound deposit to secure your place.
C: Do I need to bring anything?

🎓 Band booster

Predict before you listen: use the question to guess whether the answer is a number, a name, a time or a noun. Watch word limits ("NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER"). Spelling counts — a misspelt word scores zero, so drill names, dates and common nouns. If you miss one, let it go and stay with the recording.
