VOCABULARY
Key words · learn before you read
VERB
to binge-watch
to watch many episodes of a show one after another.
CCQ: One episode or many? Many · In one sitting? Yes
NOUN
a cliffhanger
an exciting, unfinished ending that makes you want more.
CCQ: Is the story finished? No · Want to keep watching? Yes
ADJECTIVE
gripping
so exciting and interesting that you can't stop.
CCQ: Boring or exciting? Exciting · Easy to stop? No
