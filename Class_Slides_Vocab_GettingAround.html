Getting Around & Directions · Vocabulary02 / 10
WORDS · 1
Types of roads & areas
Where you walk & drive 🛣️
street / road
a way with houses or shops along it.
avenue
a wide street, often with trees. (Sp. avenida)
boulevard
a very wide, important city avenue.
lane
a narrow road, often in the countryside.
square
an open space with buildings around it. (Sp. plaza)
block
the area / buildings between two streets. "two blocks away".
neighbourhood
the area where people live. (US neighborhood)
the city centre
the middle, busiest part of a city. (US downtown)
the outskirts
the outer edge of a town, far from the centre.
Getting Around & Directions · Vocabulary03 / 10
WORDS · 2
On the street · what you see
Features & crossings 🚦
corner
where two streets meet. "on the corner".
crossroads / junction
a place where two or more roads cross.
roundabout
a circular junction you drive around. (US traffic circle)
traffic lights
the red / amber / green lights for cars.
pedestrian crossing
a safe place to cross on foot. (zebra crossing)
pavement
the path at the side for people. (US sidewalk)
bridge
a structure over a river, road or railway.
bus stop
where you wait for the bus.
dead end
a street with no way out at the end.

WORDS · 3
Buildings & landmarks
Places you look for 🏛️
landmark
a famous, easy-to-see building or place you use to find your way.
town hall
the main local government building.
(train) station
where trains stop. Also "bus station".
car park
a place to leave your car. (US parking lot)
shopping centre
a big building with many shops. (US mall)
crossing
any place where you cross a road or railway.
the bank / café
common reference points: "opposite the bank".
park
a green public space with grass and trees.
petrol station
where cars get fuel. (US gas station)
Getting Around & Directions · Vocabulary05 / 10
LOCATION
Prepositions · where exactly?
Say where it is 📍
opposite — on the other side, facing it.
next to / beside — right at the side of.
between — in the middle of two things.
on the corner of — where two streets meet.
at the end of — at the far point of the street.
in front of / behind — ahead of it / at the back of it.
IN A SENTENCE
"The bank is opposite the park."
"It's next to the café."
"It's on the corner of Green Street."
"The station is at the end of this road."
Getting Around & Directions · Vocabulary06 / 10
DIRECTIONS
Phrases · giving the way
go straight on / ahead
don't turn — keep going forward.
turn left / right
change direction at a corner.
take the first / second turning
"…the second turning on the left."
go past
move by something without stopping.
go along / go down
follow the street. "go along this road."
cross / go over
go to the other side. "cross the road."
it's on your left / right
the destination's position as you arrive.
keep going until you reach…
continue to a point. "…until you reach the lights."
Getting Around & Directions · Vocabulary07 / 10
IN USE
Put it together · a real exchange
Asking & giving directions 💬
A: Excuse me, how do I get to the train station?
B: Go straight on and take the second turning on the left.
    Go past the bank and cross at the pedestrian crossing.
    The station is at the end of the road, on your right — you can't miss it!
A: Great, thank you! Is it far?
B: No, it's about five minutes on foot.
🎯 Useful questions: "How do I get to…?" · "Where's the nearest…?" · "Is it far?" · "Is this the right way to…?"
