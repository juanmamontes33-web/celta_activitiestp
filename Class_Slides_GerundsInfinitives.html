FORM
Two families of verbs
VERB + -ING
enjoy · avoid · mind · suggest · keep · finish · practise · can't stand · fancy · miss · imagine
"I avoid eating late."
VERB + TO + INFINITIVE
want · decide · hope · plan · manage · offer · promise · refuse · afford · agree · learn · seem
"We managed to finish."
🔑 Golden rule: after a preposition (in, at, of, about, to-as-preposition) → always -ing. "good at cooking", "look forward to seeing".

WATCH OUT
Same verb · different meaning
remember
+ to = don't forget (future)
+ -ing = a memory (past)
stop
+ -ing = quit the activity
+ to = pause in order to
try
+ to = make an effort
+ -ing = experiment
"I stopped smoking." (I quit)  ≠  "I stopped to smoke." (I paused to have one)

The rules to keep!
Gerunds vs infinitives — in a nutshell 🥜
Learn verbs in families: enjoy/avoid/keep + -ing; want/decide/manage + to.
After a preposition → always -ing.
remember / stop / try change meaning with to vs -ing.
