GERUNDS vs INFINITIVES
verb + -ing · enjoy, avoid, mind, suggest, keep
I enjoy reading. · She avoided answering.
verb + to · want, decide, hope, manage, offer
I decided to leave. · He offered to help.
👉 After a preposition, always use -ing: good at cooking, interested in learning, look forward to seeing. Some verbs change meaning: stop / remember / try + to vs -ing.
