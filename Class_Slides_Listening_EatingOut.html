MFP
Target language · Meaning · Form · Pronunciation
Get it right — M·F·P
"I'll have the salmon, please." (ordering)
MEANING = a decision made now · FORM I'll have + noun · PRON I'll /aɪl/, weak the /ðə/
"I'm allergic to nuts." (dietary)
MEANING = important safety info · FORM allergic to + noun (NOT allergic of) · PRON a·LLER·gic /əˈlɜːdʒɪk/
"Could we have the bill, please?" (paying)
MEANING = polite way to ask to pay · FORM Could we have + noun? · PRON polite fall–rise; bill (UK) / check (US)
