OVERVIEW
The four parts
What happens, part by part 🪜
Part 1 (2 min) — interview: questions about you.
Part 2 (~1 min each) — long turn: compare two photos + speculate.
Part 3 (~4 min) — collaborative task: discuss options & decide with your partner.
Part 4 (~5 min) — discussion: broader questions on the Part 3 theme.

KEY SKILL
It's a conversation, not a test
Interactive Communication 🤝
A whole criterion rewards engaging with your partner:
invite them in — "What do you think?"
respond — "That's a good point, and also…"
build on their ideas rather than delivering a monologue.

PART 2
The long turn · compare & speculate
Don't describe — speculate 🖼️
You compare two photos and answer a question about them — for ~1 minute.
Use modals of speculation: "They might be…", "It could be…", "She must be feeling…".
Compare, don't list: "Whereas the first shows…, the second…".
English from Glory · CAE · Speaking04 / 10
PART 3
The collaborative task
Discuss, then decide together 🧩
You get a prompt with written options; talk through each with your partner.
Agree & disagree politely: "I see your point, but…", "I'm not so sure…".
You don't have to agree — but you must reach a decision at the end.
