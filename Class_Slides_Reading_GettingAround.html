TARGET LANGUAGE
Target language · getting around
The directions toolkit 🧭
🙋 Asking for directions
Excuse me, how do I get to…? · Is there a … near here? · Is it far?
➡️ Giving directions
Go straight on · Turn left/right at… · It's on your left, opposite the…
🚇 Public transport
Which line/bus goes to…? · Where do I change? · Does this train stop at…?
🔁 Checking & clarifying
Sorry, could you repeat that? · So it's the second left? · Left or right?
👉 Directions use imperatives (Go, Turn, Take) · prepositions matter: on your left, at the corner, opposite the bank.

MFP
Meaning · Form · Pronunciation
Get it right — M·F·P
"How do I get to the station?" (asking)
MEANING = ask the way · FORM How do I get to + place? · PRON weak to /tə/, weak do /də/
"Turn left at the lights." (giving)
MEANING = tell the way · FORM imperative: Turn / Go / Take + … · PRON stress LEFT, LIGHTS
"So it's the second left?" (checking)
MEANING = confirm you understood · FORM So it's + …? · PRON rising / fall–rise = polite check
