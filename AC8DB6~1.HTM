Target language

Eating-out chunks

Phrases for the restaurant, with MFP: Meaning · Form · Pronunciation.

🍽️ Ordering

I'll have …
Could I get …?
For me, the …, please.
I'd like …
M say what you choose.  F I'll have / Could I get + noun.  P I'll /aɪl/; polite, friendly tone.

🤔 Asking about the menu

What do you recommend?
What's the … like?
Is the … spicy?
Does it come with …?
M get info / a suggestion.  F What's … like? = asking for a description; Does it come with + noun.  P stress the food word.

🥗 Dietary needs & requests

Do you have anything vegetarian?
I'm allergic to …
Could I have it without …?
M special requests.  F allergic to + noun (NOT allergic of); without + noun.  P al·LER·gic /əˈlɜːdʒɪk/.

💳 During & paying

Excuse me, when you get a chance …
Could we have the bill, please?
Can I get this to go?
M get attention / pay / take away.  F Could we have + noun; to go = US, takeaway = UK.  P bill (UK) / check (US).
