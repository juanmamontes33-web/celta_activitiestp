Core Summary · Pair Speaking02 / 10
ROUND 1
Work & Study · interview each other
Interview your partner 💼
ASK · THEN SWAP
What does a typical working or study day look like for you?
Which part is the most demanding? Why?
How do you organise a busy week?
IMAGINE · THEN SWAP
Where do you see yourself in five years?
If you could change one thing about your job/studies, what?
What skill would you most like to develop?
💡 Interviewer: ask a follow-up to every answer ("Why?", "Can you give an example?"). Then swap roles.

Core Summary · Pair Speaking05 / 10
ROLE-PLAY
Act it out · A & B
Play both parts — then swap roles 🎭
AT WORK
A suggests cancelling a project. B disagrees politely and offers another idea.
CUSTOMER SERVICE
A bought something broken online. B is the shop assistant — solve it.
LOST IN THE CITY
A is a lost tourist. B gives clear directions to the station.
MAKING PLANS
A invites B out. B is busy — decline politely and suggest another day.
🎯 Stay in English, react naturally, keep it going for at least 6 exchanges. Then switch A ↔ B.

Take turns · the one-minute challenge
One speaks 60 seconds, one listens ⏱️
A job I'd love to try The best purchase I've made A person I admire How technology changed my life My ideal weekend A problem worth solving
🎯 Speaker: keep going even with mistakes. Listener: at the end, ask one good follow-up question. Then swap. Use linking words: first of all · another thing is · on top of that · all in all.
Core Summary · Pair Speaking07 / 10
DEBATE
Take opposite sides · defend it
Mini-debates 💬
A argues FOR · B argues AGAINST · then switch sides!
"Working from home is better than working in an office."
"People rely on their phones too much these days."
"It's better to spend money on experiences than on things."
🗣️ Use: I totally agree… · I see your point, but… · I'm not so sure, because… · On the other hand…
Core Summary · Pair Speaking08 / 10
TOOLKIT
Fluency toolkit · keep it flowing
BUYING TIME
"That's a good question…"
"Let me think for a second…"
"Well, it depends…"
DEVELOPING IDEAS
"For example…"
"On top of that…"
"The main reason is…"
REACTING TO YOUR PARTNER
"Oh really? Why?"
"I see what you mean…"
"That's a good point."
🎯 The goal today isn't to be perfect — it's to keep the conversation going. Help your partner when they get stuck!
