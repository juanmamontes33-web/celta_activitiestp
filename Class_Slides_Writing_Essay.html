The essay ✍️
The compulsory task — and the most learnable. A clear four-paragraph plan plus the right phrases, and it almost writes itself.

THE TASK
What you're given
Read the prompt carefully 📋
"Some people think technology makes us less sociable. Do you agree?"
Notes — write about: 1 communication · 2 free time · 3 …your own idea
✅ You must cover points 1 and 2, and add your own third idea. 140–190 words.
Core 5 · THE SUMMIT · Writing: The Essay02 / 10
STRUCTURE
Four paragraphs · the plan
¶1 Introduction — paraphrase the question + say you'll look at both sides.
¶2 First point — note 1, with a reason and an example.
¶3 Second point — note 2 (or your own idea), with support.
¶4 Conclusion — summarise + give your opinion clearly.
💡 Plan for 5 minutes before you write. A clear structure alone lifts your "Organisation" mark.

LANGUAGE
Linkers & register · sound formal
USEFUL LINKERS
To begin with · Firstly
Furthermore · Moreover
On the other hand · However
For instance · such as
To sum up · Overall
AVOID (too informal)
loads of → many
kids → children
a lot of stuff → many things
don't / can't → do not / cannot
rhetorical "right?" → (cut it)
🎯 Show off Core 5 grammar: a passive, a conditional, a relative clause — graded "Language" rewards range.

Beyond the essay 🗂️

Report/Article — headed, neutral / engaging.
