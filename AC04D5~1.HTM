The chunks for this class

Functional phrases for meetings, with MFP: Meaning · Form · Pronunciation.

💬 Giving your opinion

If you ask me, …
The way I see it, …
I'd say (that) …
M give your view (personal, softened).  F phrase + comma + clause; I'd = I would.  P weak that /ðət/; stress the opinion, not the phrase.

✋ Interrupting politely

Sorry to jump in, but …
Can I just add something?
Could I come in here?
M enter the conversation without being rude.  F apology/permission + but…; modal Can/Could I…?  P polite fall–rise; stress jump IN, weak to /tə/.

👍 Agreeing

Absolutely.
That's a good point.
I couldn't agree more.
M show strong agreement.  F note: couldn't agree more = negative form, strong positive meaning!  P stress abSOLUTEly, couldn't aGREE MORE.

🤔 Disagreeing politely

I see your point, but …
I'm not so sure about that.
I'm afraid I disagree.
M disagree without offending — soften first.  F acknowledge (I see your point) + but; softener I'm afraid…  P fall–rise on the softener; stress not so SURE.
