THE SUMMIT
THIRD & MIXED CONDITIONALS
Third — unreal past (both clauses past)
If I had studied, I would have passed. (but I didn't)
Mixed — past cause → present result
If I had taken that job, I would be in Madrid now.
👉 3rd: If + past perfect, would have + V3. Mixed: keep the if-clause past perfect, but make the result would + verb (now).

⛰️ C1 Stretch — inversion & regret

C1 drops the if and inverts: "Had I known, I would have come." (= If I had known…). And to express regret about the past, use wish / if only + past perfect: "I wish I had studied harder," "If only I hadn't said that." Rewrite two sentences above with inversion.
