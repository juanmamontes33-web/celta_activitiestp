Key words · learn before you read
NOUN PHRASE
artificial intelligence
computer systems that can do tasks that usually need human thinking.
CCQ: A machine or a person? Machine · Does it copy thinking? Yes
VERB
to automate

CCQ: Person or machine does it? Machine · Usually faster? Yes
ADJECTIVE
accurate
exactly correct, with no mistakes.
CCQ: Full of mistakes? No · Can you trust it? Yes
