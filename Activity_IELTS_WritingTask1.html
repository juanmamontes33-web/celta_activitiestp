📊 Sample task + model overview
The taskThe line graph below shows the number of visitors (in millions) to three museums in London between 2010 and 2020. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Introduction (paraphrase)The line graph illustrates how many people, in millions, visited three museums in London over a ten-year period from 2010 to 2020. Overview (the key sentence — trends, no data)Overall, visitor numbers rose at all three museums over the period, with the British Museum consistently attracting the most visitors, whereas the Science Museum remained the least popular throughout. Detail (with data)In 2010, the British Museum received around 6 million visitors, a figure which climbed steadily to roughly 8 million by 2020. The Science Museum, by contrast, started at just over 2 million and increased only gradually, reaching approximately 3 million…
🎓 Band booster

To push past band 6: group data logically (don't describe every line separately), vary your language (rose / climbed / surged), and approximate precisely (just over, roughly, approximately, nearly). Never give opinions or reasons — Task 1 is pure reporting. Compare this skill with Cambridge: CAE has no Task 1, so this is a genuinely IELTS-specific habit to build.
