C1 CORE · COLLOCATION & REGISTER
The words that belong together — and when to use which
Collocation is which words naturally pair up (make a decision, heavy rain). Register is matching your words to the situation — formal or informal.
🎯 At C1, examiners reward natural word partnerships and the right level of formality. A grammatically perfect sentence can still sound "off" if the collocation is wrong.

🎓 Where it scores

Collocation lifts Lexical Resource in both IELTS and CAE. In CAE Use of English Part 1, the answer is almost always a collocation. Register matters most in writing: an IELTS essay and a CAE essay are formal, so swap get, a lot of and phrasal verbs for obtain, a significant number of and single formal verbs.
