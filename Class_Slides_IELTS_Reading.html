Why reading is a race

SKILL 1
Skimming · the gist
Read fast for the big picture 🚀
Read the title, first & last paragraphs, and the first sentence of each paragraph.
Goal: know what each paragraph is about — not the detail.
Spend 2–3 minutes before you look at the questions.
English from Glory · IELTS · Reading03 / 10
SKILL 2
Scanning · the detail
Hunt for one piece of information 🎯
Take key words from the question (names, dates, numbers, unusual nouns).
Move your eyes quickly down the text looking only for those — like finding a name in a phone list.
Questions usually follow the order of the text.

TFNG
True / False / Not Given
The rule that wins bands ⚖️
TRUE — the passage agrees with the statement.
FALSE — the passage says the opposite.
NOT GIVEN — the passage doesn't say. No information.
⚠️ Never use your own knowledge. If it isn't in the text, it's NOT GIVEN.
English from Glory · IELTS · Reading05 / 10
THE TRAP
False vs Not Given
The difference in one example 🔑
Text: "The museum opened in 1952 and is free to enter."
"The museum charges an entry fee." → FALSE (text says free).
"The museum is the oldest in the city." → NOT GIVEN (text never says).
