The restaurant toolkit 🍽️

🤔 About the menu

🥗 Dietary & requests
Do you have anything vegetarian? · I'm allergic to… · Could I have it without…?
💳 During & paying
Excuse me, when you get a chance… · Could we have the bill, please? · Can I get this to go?
👉 Watch out: allergic to (not of) · Does it come with…?

MFP
Target language · Meaning · Form · Pronunciation
Get it right — M·F·P
"I'll have the steak, please." (ordering)
MEANING = a decision made now · FORM I'll have + noun (not I'm having to order) · PRON I'll /aɪl/, add please to be polite
"I'm allergic to nuts." (dietary)
MEANING = my body reacts badly · FORM allergic to + noun (NOT allergic of) · PRON al·LER·gic /əˈlɜːdʒɪk/
"What do you recommend?" (asking)
MEANING = asking for a suggestion · FORM do you recommend (base verb, no -ing) · PRON reco·MMEND /ˌrekəˈmend/
