💡 In defining clauses, you can drop the pronoun when it's the object: "the film (that) I love".

KEY CONCEPT
The comma rule · essential vs extra
DEFINING · NO COMMAS
"The actor who plays the villain is Colombian."
The clause is essential — it tells us which actor. Remove it and the sentence loses its meaning.
NON-DEFINING · COMMAS
"Pedro Pascal, who is Chilean, is a huge star."
The clause is extra — nice to know, but not essential. Commas act like brackets. (No "that" here!)
🧠 CCQ: If you remove the clause, do you still know exactly who/what? Yes → commas (extra). No → no commas (essential).

TAKEAWAY
The rules to keep!
Relative clauses — in a nutshell 🥜
who people · which things · that both (defining) · where places · whose possession · when times
Essential info → no commas, and that is fine.
Extra info → commas, and no that.
