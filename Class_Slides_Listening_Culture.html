VOCABULARY
Key words · learn before you listen
NOUN
an open mic night
an event where anyone can get up and perform.
CCQ: Only professionals? No · Can anyone try? Yes
VERB / NOUN
to busk
to play music in the street for money. (a busker)
CCQ: Inside or in public? In public · Do they get paid? Yes
IDIOM
take sb under your wing
to protect and guide someone less experienced.
CCQ: Help or ignore them? Help · Older/wiser person? Usually
💬 Also useful: a gig (an informal word for a concert) · your main earner (your main source of income).

DISCUSSION
Group it out · agree or disagree?
"Anyone can become a musician." 🎙️
AGREE 👍
Open mics, busking and the internet mean anyone can start. It's about practice and courage, not luck.
DISAGREE 👎
You need talent, money and the right connections. Most musicians never earn a living from it.
