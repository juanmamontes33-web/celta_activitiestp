VOCABULARY
Key words · before you listen
NOUN · /ɪnˈsɒmniæk/
an insomniac
a person who finds it very hard to sleep.
CCQ: Do they sleep easily? No · At night, awake? Yes
VERB / NOUN
to regret
to feel sorry about something you did or bought.
CCQ: Happy or sorry? Sorry · Before or after? After
PHRASE
to get sucked in
to become so involved that you can't stop easily.
CCQ: Easy to stop? No · Do you keep going? Yes
