LEAD-IN
Grammatically right, still wrong
Which sounds native? 🤔
"I did a big mistake and took a strong decision."
Every word is a real word — but the partners are wrong.
"I made a serious mistake and took a firm decision."
💡 Collocation is knowing which words habitually travel together. You learn it in chunks, not rules.

STRENGTH
Strong vs weak collocation
Some pairings are fixed ⚡
Strong — few alternatives: "a foregone conclusion", "drive someone mad".
Weak — many options: "a nice/good/great day".
🎯 Strong collocations impress most — but only if they're accurate. When unsure, choose a safe, natural pairing.

HOW TO LEARN
Build your collocation bank
Record words in chunks 🏦
Don't note "decision" alone — note "make / take / reach a decision".
Keep a collocation notebook by topic (environment, technology, health).
When you read, underline the pairings, not just new single words.

WATCH OUT
Common errors · Spanish L1
False friends & direct translation 🚫
✗ "make a party" → ✓ have / throw a party (hacer ≠ always make)
✗ "assist to the meeting" → ✓ attend the meeting (asistir false friend)
✗ "take a decision" is British-OK, but ✓ make a decision is safer globally.
