MFP
Target language · Meaning · Form · Pronunciation
Get it right — M·F·P
"I'm going to follow my passion." (a plan)
MEANING = a decision already made · FORM be going to + base verb · PRON fast speech: going to → "gonna" /ˈɡʌnə/
"I'd like to do work I love." (a wish)
MEANING = a hope, not certain · FORM 'd like / hope + to + base verb · PRON I'd /aɪd/, weak to /tə/
"I see myself changing the world." (a vision)
MEANING = how you picture your future · FORM see myself + -ing (NOT to change) · PRON stress the goal
