TARGET LANGUAGE
Target language · future forms
Talking about the future 🔮
💭 Dreams & ambitions
I'd like to… · I hope to… · My dream is to… · I've always wanted to…
📋 Plans & intentions
I'm going to… · I'm planning to… · I intend to… · I'm starting … next month
🔮 Predictions & goals
I think I'll… · I'll probably… · In five years, I'll…
🎯 Seeing your future
I see myself …-ing… · I aim to… · I'm working towards…
👉 Notice: hope/plan/'d like + to + verb, but see myself + -ing!
Target language · present & drill07 / 10
MFP
Target language · Meaning · Form · Pronunciation
Get it right — M·F·P
"I'm going to study medicine." (a plan)
MEANING = a decision already made · FORM be going to + base verb · PRON fast speech: going to → "gonna" /ˈɡʌnə/
"I'd like to work abroad." (a wish)
MEANING = a hope, not certain · FORM 'd like / hope + to + base verb · PRON I'd /aɪd/, weak to /tə/
"I see myself leading a team." (a vision)
MEANING = how you picture your future · FORM see myself + -ing (NOT to lead) · PRON stress LEADing a TEAM
