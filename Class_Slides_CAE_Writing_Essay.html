The compulsory Essay ✍️
Discuss two given points, support them, and give your opinion — 220–260 words of controlled, formal English.

THE TASK
What you're given
Read the prompt carefully 📋
Typical prompt:
"Your class has discussed how cities can cut traffic. Write an essay discussing two of these ways — public transport / cycle lanes / congestion charges — and say which is most effective."
⚠️ You choose two points to develop — but you must give your own view on which is best.

STRUCTURE
The four paragraphs
A clear, balanced shape 🧱
Introduction — rephrase the topic + say what the essay will do.
Body 1 — develop the first point with a reason / example.
Body 2 — develop the second point with a reason / example.
Conclusion — say which is more important, and why.

INTRO
Open without copying
Rephrase, then preview 🎬
"Traffic congestion is a growing problem in many cities, and authorities are under pressure to address it. This essay will consider two possible solutions before concluding which is more effective."
💡 Don't state your full opinion yet — preview the discussion; save the verdict for the conclusion.
English from Glory · CAE · The Essay04 / 10
BODY
Develop each point
Point → reason → result 🔗
Point — "Firstly, investing in public transport has a significant impact."
Reason — "When buses are frequent and affordable, commuters leave their cars at home."
Result — "As a result, the number of vehicles on the road falls."
🎯 Depth over breadth: two well-developed points beat four thin ones.

MARKING
The four criteria
How it's scored 🏆

Communicative Achievement — right register & essay conventions.
Organisation — paragraphs, linking, logical flow.
Language — range & accuracy of grammar and vocabulary.

WATCH OUT
Common errors · Spanish L1
Fix these 🚫
✗ "In my opinion I think…" → ✓ In my opinion, … (don't double)
✗ "It's cheaper and don't pollute." → ✓ …and does not pollute. (no contractions)
✗ "Actually" for "currently" → ✓ currently / at present (false friend)
