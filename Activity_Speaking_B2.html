🎤 Live practice — speaking prompts

Part 1 · Interview (2–3 min)
Answer in 2–3 sentences — don't just say "yes". Add a reason or example.
  • Where are you from, and what's it like to live there?
  • Do you work or study? Tell us about it.
  • What do you enjoy doing in your free time?
  • What are your plans for the next few years?
Part 2 · Long turn (1 min each)
Candidate A: compare the two situations and say why the people might have chosen them. Candidate B: answer the short follow-up (30s).
  • Photo A: a group of students studying together in a library.
  • Photo B: a person learning online alone at home.
Follow-up for B: Which way of studying would you prefer?
Part 3 · Collaborative task (2–3 min)
Talk together: "How can people improve their English?" Discuss each option, then decide which TWO are the most effective.

Part 4 · Discussion (3–4 min)
  • Is it better to learn a language as a child or as an adult? Why?
  • Do you think technology will ever replace language teachers?
  • Why do some people find speaking harder than writing?
⛰️ C1 Stretch — toward C1 Advanced Speaking

At C1 the long turn compares three pictures (selecting, not describing all), the collaborative task is more abstract, and Part 4 answers run longer. Aim for more precise, hedged language: "it's conceivable that…", "arguably…", "that rather depends on…", "I'd be inclined to say…". Re-do Part 3 above using two of these.
