📄 Model proposal (extract) — reveal
Task: The college principal has asked for proposals on improving the student common room.

Introduction
The purpose of this proposal is to suggest ways of improving the common room so that it better meets students' needs. It is based on comments gathered from fellow students.

Current problems
At present, the room is poorly furnished and lacks facilities. As a result, few students choose to spend their breaks there.

Recommendations
I would strongly recommend installing comfortable seating and a small kitchen area. In addition, access to Wi-Fi would make the space suitable for group study.

Conclusion
If these changes were implemented, the common room would become a far more welcoming environment, which would benefit the whole college.
🎓 Exam note

Proposal vs report: a proposal argues for future action (persuasive); a report presents findings (neutral). Both use headings. A review is more engaging and personal, and always ends with a clear recommendation. Identify the reader (principal, committee, magazine readers) and pitch your register accordingly.
