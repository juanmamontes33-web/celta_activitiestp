TARGET LANGUAGE
Target language · email chunks
The email toolkit ✉️
📨 Opening & purpose
Dear Mr/Ms…, · Hi…, · I hope you're well. · I'm writing to…
🙏 Polite requests
Could you please…? · I was wondering if you could… · Would it be possible to…?
📎 Attachments
Please find attached… · I've attached… · As mentioned,…
🔁 Follow-up & closing
I'm just following up on… · I look forward to hearing from you. · Best regards,
👉 Watch out: I'm writing to + verb but look forward to + -ing!
Target language · present & drill07 / 10
MFP
Target language · Meaning · Form · Pronunciation
Get it right — M·F·P
"I'm writing to ask about…" (purpose)
MEANING = why you're emailing · FORM I'm writing to + base verb (NOT writing for ask) · PRON weak to /tə/
"I look forward to hearing from you." (closing)
MEANING = a polite ending · FORM look forward to + -ing (to is a preposition here!) · PRON "look forward to HEARing"
"Please find attached the report." (attachment)
MEANING = here is the file · FORM find attached + noun (fixed phrase) · PRON at·TACHED /əˈtætʃt/
