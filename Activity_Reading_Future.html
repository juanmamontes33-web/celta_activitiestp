Target language

Talking about the future

Future forms for dreams, jobs & plans, with MFP: Meaning · Form · Pronunciation.

💭 Dreams & ambitions

I'd like to …
I hope to …
My dream is to …
I've always wanted to …
M a wish / hope (not certain).  F 'd like / hope / want + to + base verb.  P I'd /aɪd/; weak to /tə/.

📋 Plans & intentions

I'm going to …
I'm planning to …
I intend to …
I'm starting … next month
M a decision already made.  F be going to / be planning to + base; present continuous for arrangements.  P going to → "gonna" in fast speech.

🔮 Predictions & goals

I think I'll …
I'll probably …
In five years, I'll …
M a prediction / less-fixed future.  F will ('ll) + base verb.  P contraction 'll /l/; probably /ˈprɒbli/.

🎯 Seeing your future

I see myself + -ing …
I aim to …
I'm working towards …
M your vision / goal.  F see myself + -ing (gerund!); aim to + base; working towards + noun.  P stress the goal word.
