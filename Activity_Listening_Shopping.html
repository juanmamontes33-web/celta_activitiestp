📄 Transcript — teacher reference / post-listening

Neil & Rob: Rob doesn't enjoy walking around shopping malls — he finds it tiring and would rather shop online from the comfort of home. Buying things online is now big business.

The topic: "Vampire shopping" means shopping late at night — a time when most people are asleep. But sleep-deprived parents, shift-workers, gamers and insomniacs (people who can't sleep easily) are often awake. Online shops are open 24 hours a day, so it's easy to get sucked in and keep shopping.

The downside: Shopping when we're tired is sometimes too easy, and we make purchases we later regret — regret is the sad feeling you get when you wish you hadn't done something. Luckily, there's usually the option to return an item and get a refund.

Research mentioned: A survey found that around three-quarters of people did not send back the last thing they bought online that they regretted. As online shopping keeps growing, the temptation will always be there.
