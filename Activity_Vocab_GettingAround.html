📖 Key vocabulary — glossary
ROADS & AREAS

ON THE STREET
corner · crossroads / junction · roundabout (US traffic circle) · traffic lights · pedestrian crossing · pavement (US sidewalk) · bridge · bus stop · dead end
BUILDINGS & LANDMARKS
landmark · town hall · (train) station · car park (US parking lot) · shopping centre (US mall) · crossing · bank · café · park · petrol station (US gas station)
WHERE (prepositions)

GIVING DIRECTIONS

USEFUL QUESTIONS
