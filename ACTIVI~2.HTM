📄 Transcript — teacher reference / read aloud

Maya (host): Welcome back to The Wandering Ear. Today I'm with travel writer Tom Rivers, who spent a whole month in Vietnam. Tom, what made this trip different?

Tom: Well, normally I try to cram in as many places as possible, but this time I decided to slow down. I spent two whole weeks in a single town called Hoi An.

Maya: Two weeks in one place! Weren't you bored?

Tom: Not at all. Because I stayed longer, I got to know the locals. I ate at the same little family-run restaurant every morning, and by the end the owner knew my name. That never happens when you're rushing.

Maya: What was the highlight?

Tom: Honestly, the smallest moment. One evening I was cycling back to my guesthouse when it started to rain. A farmer invited me in, we shared tea, and I picked up a few words of Vietnamese. No landmark could beat that.

Maya: And the cost?

Tom: Much cheaper than my usual trips. I rented a room by the month and cooked at home. I probably spent less in a month than I usually spend in a week.

Maya: Any advice for our listeners?

Tom: Don't try to see everything. Choose one place, stay longer, and let things happen. You'll come back with better stories — and more money in your pocket.

Maya: Wise words. Tom Rivers, thank you.
