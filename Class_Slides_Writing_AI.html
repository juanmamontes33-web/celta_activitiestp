THE TASK
What you're going to write
A for-and-against essay 🧾
EXAM-STYLE QUESTION
"Some people say we should not trust artificial intelligence. Discuss both sides of this view and give your own opinion."
🎯 Goal: present both sides fairly, in a formal, organised way — then give your view at the end.
📏 Length: 140–190 words · 4 paragraphs · about 40 minutes.
Core 3 · Technology & The World · Writing03 / 10
MODEL
Read the model · notice the shape
¶1 Artificial intelligence is now part of everyday life, from phones to hospitals. However, many people wonder whether we can really trust it. This essay will look at both sides.

¶2 On the one hand, there are good reasons to trust AI. It is fast, accurate and never gets tired, so it can do many tasks better than humans. For instance, computers already help doctors diagnose illnesses more quickly.

¶3 On the other hand, AI also has clear weaknesses. Firstly, it learns from human data, so it can repeat our mistakes. Furthermore, it has no common sense, which means it can be confidently wrong.

¶4 In conclusion, I believe we should use AI but not trust it completely. In my view, the best approach is to let machines help us while humans make the final decisions.
Core 3 · Technology & The World · Writing04 / 10
STRUCTURE
Four paragraphs · the plan
¶1 · INTRODUCTION
Rephrase the topic. Say you will look at both sides. Don't give your opinion yet.
¶2 · ARGUMENTS FOR
Two reasons to trust AI, each with a short example.
¶3 · ARGUMENTS AGAINST
Two reasons to be careful, again with support or examples.
¶4 · CONCLUSION
Now give your opinion, clearly and briefly. No new arguments.
🎯 One idea per paragraph body = one clear point. Balance: give the two sides roughly equal space.

Core 3 · Technology & The World · Writing06 / 10
REGISTER
Keep it formal · the essay voice
Write it like an essay, not a chat 🎩
✗ TOO INFORMAL
"AI's pretty cool but it's kinda scary too."
"I think it's a big problem, tbh."
"And another thing…"
✓ FORMAL & CLEAR
"AI offers clear benefits, but it also raises concerns."
"It could be argued that…"
"Furthermore, …"
🎯 No contractions (it is, not it's) · no slang · no "I think it's cool" — use full sentences and linkers.
