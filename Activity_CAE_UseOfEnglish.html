Part 1 — Multiple-choice cloze 🎯
Nuanced vocabulary & collocation — only one word truly fits. A, B, C or D.
Part 2 — Open cloze 🔑
One word per gap — C1 fixed phrases, inversions and grammar words.
Part 3 — Word formation 🔨
Change the ROOT — watch for negative prefixes (un-, in-, im-) and internal changes.
Part 4 — Key word transformations ⭐
Rewrite with the KEY word, using 3–6 words. Often combines two structures.
🎓 Exam note

CAE Reading & Use of English is 90 minutes, 8 parts. These four Use-of-English parts reward the C1 core skills directly: Part 1 tests collocation & nuance, Part 4 tests inversion, hedging and fixed phrases (the structures from this module). Name the grammar point before you build each transformation.
