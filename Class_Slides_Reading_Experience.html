TARGET LANGUAGE
Target language · the chunks
Interview-ready chunks 🗣️
🧩 Responsibilities
I'm responsible for… · My role involves… · I'm in charge of… · Part of my job is to…
🏆 Achievements
I managed to… · I successfully… · One of my proudest achievements was… · I increased/improved…
⏳ Experience
I have X years' experience in… · I've worked as a… for… · I've been …-ing since/for…
⚡ Power verbs
led · developed · launched · managed · increased · improved · achieved
👉 Drill the chunks, then swap the words for your own job.
Target language · present & drill07 / 10
MFP
Target language · Meaning · Form · Pronunciation
Get it right — M·F·P
"I'm responsible for + noun/-ing" (responsibilities)
MEANING = it's my job to do this · FORM responsible for + noun/-ing (NOT responsible of) · PRON weak for /fə/
"I managed to increase sales by 20%." (achievement)
MEANING = I succeeded in doing it · FORM managed to + base verb; past + a number · PRON stress the number TWENTY per cent
"I've worked as a nurse for five years." (experience)
MEANING = started in the past, still true · FORM present perfect + for (period) / since (point) · PRON -ed: worked /t/, managed /d/, started /ɪd/
