VOCABULARY
Key words · before you listen
NOUN · /ræˈpɔː/
rapport
a good connection and understanding between people.
CCQ: Good or bad connection? Good · Do they understand each other? Yes
ADJECTIVE
frank
open and honest, even about difficult things.
CCQ: Honest? Yes · Hides the truth? No
ADJECTIVE
sarcastic
saying the opposite of what you mean, often to mock.
CCQ: "Oh, great…" (angry) — do they mean it? No
