PART 5
Multiple choice · the paraphrase trap
The answer is a paraphrase 🎯
The correct option restates the text in different words. The wrong options often repeat words from the text — a trap. Don't "word-spot"; match the meaning.
💡 Read the question, predict the answer in your head, then find the option that matches your prediction.

PART 7
Gapped text · follow the thread
Use the glue words 🧩
A paragraph fits a gap when it connects to what comes before AND after. Track reference words (this, these, such, he, the latter) and linkers (however, as a result).
⚠️ There is always one extra paragraph you don't use. Don't force it.

PART 8
Multiple matching · scan, don't read
Questions first, then hunt 🔎
You match each statement to a section (A–F). Read the questions first, then scan the sections for the paraphrased information — you do not need to read every word in order.
💡 Sections can be used more than once; some statements look similar — check carefully.

SKILL
Deduce meaning from context
You don't need every word 📚
C1 readers guess unknown words from context. Ask: is it positive or negative? What part of speech? What would make sense here?
"The model may not translate to every sector" → here translate = apply / work, not convert a language.
