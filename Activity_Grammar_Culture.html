RELATIVE CLAUSES
Defining — no commas (essential info)
"The singer who wrote this song is only 19."
Non-defining — commas (extra info)
"Adele, who is from London, has won 16 Grammys."
👉 who (people) · which (things) · that (people/things, defining only) · where (places) · whose (possession) · when (times)
