TARGET LANGUAGE
Target language · the chunks
Say it like this 🗣️
💬 Giving your opinion
If you ask me, … · The way I see it, … · I'd say (that)…
✋ Interrupting politely
Sorry to jump in, but… · Can I just add something? · Could I come in here?
👍 Agreeing
Absolutely. · That's a good point. · I couldn't agree more.
🤔 Disagreeing politely
I see your point, but… · I'm not so sure about that. · I'm afraid I disagree.
👉 Drill each chunk chorally, then in pairs — learn them whole.

MFP
Target language · Meaning · Form · Pronunciation
Get it right — M·F·P
"I couldn't agree more." (strong agreement)
MEANING = I totally agree · FORM negative form, positive meaning (a trap!) · PRON stress aGREE MORE, weak couldn't /ˈkʊdnt/
"Sorry to jump in, but…" (interrupting)
MEANING = polite way to interrupt · FORM apology + to + verb + but… · PRON weak to /tə/, stress JUMP in, fall–rise = polite
"I'm afraid I disagree." (disagreeing softly)
MEANING = I disagree (softened) · FORM I'm afraid = softener, NOT fear · PRON weak I'm afraid, stress disaGREE
