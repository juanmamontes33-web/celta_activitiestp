📄 Reference — teacher notes / post-listening

Hosts (Pippa & Neil): The programme is about people across the UK who want to become successful musicians. Neil has played the guitar since he was 14 and still dreams of being a musician; Pippa says she wouldn't want the stress of being famous with lots of fans.

Bailey Tomkinson (St Ives, Cornwall): She started going to open mic nights — events where anyone can perform — at around age 11, then joined songwriters' circles (meetings for people who write their own music). The older members took her under their wing (protected and guided her), and artists like James Taylor, Carole King and The Eagles shaped her sound. She was the youngest there by about 50 years.

Josh Robinson (London): He learned his first proper song at about 13 and started busking (performing in the street for money) straight away. Busking has been his main earner (main source of income) for about ten years and has brought him gigs (concerts) and useful connections. He says he owes a lot to busking.

Quiz fact: The artist with the most UK number-one songs is Elvis Presley (21), ahead of The Beatles (18).
