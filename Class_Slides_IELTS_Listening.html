SKILL 1
Predict before you listen
Use the gap to guess 🔮
Before the audio, read each question and ask: is the answer a number, a name, a time, a place or a noun?
"The deposit is £____" → you're listening for a number.
"Bring a ____" → you're listening for an object.
English from Glory · IELTS · Listening03 / 10
ACCURACY
Numbers & spelling count
Get the detail exactly right ✍️
A misspelt word scores zero — drill names, days, months, common nouns.
Watch "double" and "triple" in phone numbers (double-0 = 00).
Dates, times and currencies must be written precisely.

MAPS
Labelling a map
Direction & position language 🧭
opposite · next to · between
behind · in front of · beside
go straight ahead · turn left/right
across / over · around the corner
💡 Keep your finger on the "you are here" point and follow the speaker's directions in real time.
English from Glory · IELTS · Listening05 / 10
THE TRAP
They won't say the exact words
Listen for meaning, not words 👂
The question says "the course is free" — the speaker says "there's no charge".
Listen for paraphrase and synonyms, and beware "correction" — "It's on Monday… sorry, Tuesday."

WATCH OUT
Follow the instructions
Word limits & transfer time ⏱️
"NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS" — three words scores zero.
On paper you get 10 minutes to transfer answers; on computer you don't.
Never leave a blank — guess if unsure.
English from Glory · IELTS · Listening08 / 10
BEYOND CLASS
Train your ear
Daily listening habits 🎙️
Listen to podcasts & the news in English daily — a range of accents.
Practise dictation of numbers, dates and spellings.
Do timed practice tests — one listen only, like the real exam.
