How C1 differs from B2
Same shape, higher bar 🔼
• Collocations are subtler — fixed phrases, not obvious words.
• Word formation needs prefixes & internal changes (accept → unacceptable).
• Transformations run to 3–6 words and combine structures (e.g. inversion + a fixed phrase).

PART 1
Multiple-choice cloze · nuance
Part 1 — the one word that fits 🎯
All four options may look possible — only one collocates. Tests fixed phrases, phrasal verbs and shades of meaning.
"The findings cast doubt on the theory." (not made / did / put doubt)
💡 Read the words around the gap — the collocation partner is the clue.

Part 2 — fixed phrases & grammar 🔑
One word per gap. At C1, expect fixed expressions and inversions, not just prepositions.
"No sooner had we arrived than…" · "But for your help…" · "It's high time we did…"

PART 3
Word formation · build the word
Part 3 — prefixes & internal changes 🔨
Change the ROOT — but at C1 you often need a negative prefix or a spelling change.
ACCEPT → unacceptable · COMPATIBLE → incompatible · DIVERSE → diversity
💡 Ask: part of speech? positive or negative? singular or plural? Then check the spelling.

Key word transformations · the big one
Part 4 — 3–6 words, combined ⭐
1st sentence:
"As soon as we sat down, the show began."
KEY WORD: SOONER →
"No sooner had we sat down than the show began."

STRATEGY
Score more marks
Habits that win marks 🏆
Count words in Part 4 (3–6). Contractions count as two.
Learn collocations in chunks — "cast doubt", "pose a threat", "fall through".
In Part 3, keep a prefix list (un-, in-, im-, dis-, mis-) ready.
Never leave blanks · check spelling — a misspelt answer scores zero.
