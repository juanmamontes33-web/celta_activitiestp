Exam task: "Some people believe cities should reduce traffic by improving public transport, while others prefer building cycle lanes. Discuss both and say which is more effective."
📄 Model essay — reveal
Introduction: Traffic congestion is a growing problem in many cities, and authorities are under increasing pressure to address it. This essay will consider two possible solutions — improving public transport and building cycle lanes — before concluding which is likely to be more effective.

Body 1: Firstly, investing in public transport can have a significant impact. When buses and trains are frequent and affordable, commuters are far more willing to leave their cars at home. As a result, the overall number of vehicles on the road falls.

Body 2: Building cycle lanes, on the other hand, encourages a healthier and cleaner alternative. However, its effect is somewhat limited by weather and distance, and it appeals mainly to those making shorter journeys.

Conclusion: In conclusion, while both measures are valuable, improving public transport is, in my view, the more effective solution, since it serves a far greater number of travellers.
🎓 Exam note

CAE essays reward range and control. Use the core-skills language directly: hedging ("this may be because…"), emphasis ("Not only does it… but…"), and strong collocations. Plan 5 minutes, write ~230 words, and leave a minute to check. Address both points — ignoring one caps your Content mark.
