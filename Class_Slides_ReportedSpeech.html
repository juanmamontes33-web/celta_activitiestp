The backshift · one step back
present → past
am/is → was

past → past perfect

💡 If the fact is still true, backshift is optional: "She said she is / was Colombian." Both fine.

KEY POINTS
say / tell · questions · commands
SAY vs TELL
✅ He told me he was late.
✅ He said he was late.
❌ He said me / told that
tell + person · say (no person)
QUESTIONS
"Where do you live?"
→ She asked where I lived.
Yes/No → if / whether
No do/does/did · normal word order
COMMANDS
"Wait!" → She told me to wait.
"Don't go!" → He told me not to go.
tell sb (not) + to + verb

The rules to keep!
