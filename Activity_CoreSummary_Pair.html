Complete & Say ✍️🗣️
Take turns: A does the odd numbers, B does the even numbers. Fill the gap, then read the whole sentence aloud to your partner.
Correct & Say ✅🗣️
Work together: spot the crossed-out mistake, agree on the fix, type it, then each say the correct sentence aloud.
Interview Each Other 🎤
No typing. A interviews B through one group, then swap roles for the next. Ask a follow-up to every answer!
A asks Bthen swap → B asks A
💼 WORK & STUDY
  • What are the main responsibilities in your work or studies?
  • Tell me about a time something went wrong — what did you do?
  • What are your plans for the next few months?
Follow-ups: Why? · Can you give an example? · How did that feel?
🛒 EVERYDAY LIFE
  • Describe your perfect day off, from morning to night.
  • Talk about a person who has influenced you.
  • Tell me about something you bought that was (or wasn't) worth it.
Find one thing in common and report it back to the teacher.
💬 OPINIONS
  • Do you think people read enough these days? Why / why not?
  • Is it better to live in a big city or a small town?
  • Should everyone learn a second language? Defend your answer.
If you disagree, say so politely: I see your point, but…
Talk for a Minute ⏱️
One of you presses for a topic and speaks 60 seconds; the other listens and asks a follow-up at the end. Then swap.
🎲 Press "New topic" to begin.
