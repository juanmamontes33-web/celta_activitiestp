Writing Task 1 — describing data 📊
Report what a graph shows — trends, comparisons, key figures — in 150 words and 20 minutes. No opinions. This task does not exist in Cambridge, so it's pure IELTS.
English from Glory · IELTS · Writing Task 101 / 10
LEAD-IN
What it is · what it isn't
DO ✅
Describe the main trends · compare figures · group data logically · use accurate data language
DON'T ❌
Give opinions · explain why · describe every single number · write "I think"
💬 You'll see a graph showing phone sales over 5 years. In one sentence, what's the single biggest thing a reader should notice? That instinct is your overview.
English from Glory · IELTS · Writing Task 102 / 10
STRUCTURE
Four short paragraphs
¶1 Introduction — paraphrase the question (don't copy it).
¶2 Overview — the 2 biggest trends, no numbers. Start with "Overall,…".
¶3 Details — one group of data, with figures.
¶4 Details — the other group of data, with figures.
💡 The overview is worth the most. Examiners look for it first — never skip it.
English from Glory · IELTS · Writing Task 103 / 10
LANGUAGE
The data toolkit
UP
rose · climbed · increased · surged · soared
DOWN
fell · declined · dropped · plummeted · dipped
NO CHANGE
remained stable · levelled off · plateaued
HOW MUCH
sharply · dramatically · gradually · slightly
APPROX.
just over · roughly · around · nearly
PREPOSITIONS
rose by 10% · rose to $50 · at 40%
🔑 Noun forms too: "a sharp rise", "a gradual decline", "a steady increase" — vary verb and noun phrasings.
English from Glory · IELTS · Writing Task 104 / 10
KEY SENTENCE
The overview · your highest-value line
Write the overview first ⭐
MODEL
"Overall, visitor numbers rose at all three museums, with the British Museum consistently the most popular, whereas the Science Museum remained the least visited."
🎯 Two big trends · a comparison · no specific numbers. Begin "Overall," or "In general,".

English from Glory · IELTS · Writing Task 107 / 10
MODEL
How it fits together
INTRO
The line graph illustrates the number of visitors to three London museums from 2010 to 2020.
OVERVIEW
Overall, numbers rose at all three, with the British Museum the most popular throughout.
DETAIL
In 2010, the British Museum received around 6 million visitors, a figure which climbed steadily to roughly 8 million by 2020.

English from Glory · IELTS · Writing Task 108 / 10
BAND 7+
What lifts your band
Four band-boosters 🏆
A clear overview — the #1 thing examiners check.
Group data logically — don't list every line separately.
Vary language — verbs + nouns, and accurate prepositions (by/to/at).
Never give reasons or opinions — that belongs in Task 2.
