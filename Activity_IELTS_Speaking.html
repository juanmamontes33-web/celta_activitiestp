Parts 1–3 — the interview, the long turn, the discussion
IELTS Speaking is a face-to-face interview, 11–14 minutes, in three parts. You're scored on Fluency & Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range and Pronunciation.
🎯 The golden habit: never give a one-word answer. Always extend with a reason, an example or a contrast.

Extend the answer 🌱

The long turn (Part 2) 🎤
You get 1 minute to prepare and speak for 1–2 minutes. Complete the cue-card language.
Cue card: Describe a skill you would like to learn. You should say: what the skill is · why you want to learn it · how you would learn it, and explain how it would be useful to you.
🎓 Band booster

Fluency: use fillers ("that's an interesting question…") instead of silence. Lexical Resource: bring in collocations and the hedging language from your core skills. Part 2: use your minute to note keywords, not sentences, and cover all four bullet points. Don't memorise answers — examiners spot it instantly.
