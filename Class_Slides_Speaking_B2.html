The speaking test 🎤
14 minutes, four parts, a partner and two examiners. Know the shape, learn the phrases, and let your Core 5 grammar shine out loud.

THE FORMAT
What happens · 14 minutes
Part 1 · Interview — the examiner asks you both personal questions. (2 min)
Part 2 · Long turn — you speak alone for 1 minute, comparing two photos. (4 min)
Part 3 · Collaborative task — you and your partner discuss options and decide. (4 min)
Part 4 · Discussion — broader questions on the same topic. (4 min)
💡 You're graded with your partner, not against them — help the conversation flow.
Core 5 · THE SUMMIT · Speaking02 / 10
PART 1
Interview · make a good start
Part 1 — extend every answer ➕
Personal questions about you — home, work/study, free time, future. Never answer in one word.
❌ "Yes."
✅ "Yes, I do. I'm a teacher, which I love because every class is different."
🎯 Formula: answer + reason + example. Slip in a relative clause or a comparative — show range.
Core 5 · THE SUMMIT · Speaking03 / 10
PART 2
Long turn · compare + speculate
Part 2 — one minute, two photos 🖼️
Don't describe every detail. Compare the two, then speculate about the people and answer the question on the task.
"Both show people learning, but whereas the first is a group, the second is alone. They might prefer…"
⏱️ Keep going for the full minute — if you dry up, speculate more. Use might / could / probably / it looks as if.

PART 3
Collaborative task · discuss + decide
Part 3 — talk with your partner 🤝
A question with five options. Discuss each one together (~2 min), then the examiner asks you to decide (~1 min).
"Shall we start with this one? … I see your point, but… So, do we agree that…?"
🎯 Interactive Communication is graded here: ask your partner's view, build on their ideas, and negotiate — don't make speeches.
Core 5 · THE SUMMIT · Speaking05 / 10
PART 4
Discussion · go deeper
Part 4 — opinions & reasons 💬
Broader questions linked to Part 3. Give an opinion, a reason and an example — and react to your partner's view.
"In my view… — mainly because… For instance… What about you?"
🎯 Here's where Core 5 pays off: use conditionals ("If people…"), modals and reported speech to sound C1-ready.

PRACTICE
Open the activity · then speak
Drill the phrases, then do it live 🎤
Tabs 1–4 lock in the functional language. Then use the prompt cards at the bottom of the activity to run all four parts in pairs — time each part.
🔁 Swap roles and repeat. Record yourselves if you can — listening back is the fastest way to improve.
