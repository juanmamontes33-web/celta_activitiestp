The four parts 🎯
Know what each part asks of you — and your partner.
Speculating language 🔑
Part 2 asks you to speculate about photos. Complete each phrase with one word.
Collaborating language 🤝
Part 3 is about interacting — agreeing, disagreeing and deciding together. Complete the phrase.
Part 3 task: "Here are some things that make a city a good place to live. Talk together about how important each one is, then decide which two matter most." (green spaces · public transport · job opportunities · safety · culture)
Compare the photos 🖼️
Build the Part 2 long turn — compare, then speculate. Complete each sentence.
Part 2 task: Compare these two photos of people learning something new, and say how the people might be feeling.
🎓 Exam note

Interactive Communication is a whole criterion: invite your partner in ("What do you think?"), respond to their ideas, and build on them — don't deliver a monologue. In Part 2, speculate with modals (might, could, must) rather than describing every detail. In Part 3, you don't have to agree — but you must reach a decision at the end.
