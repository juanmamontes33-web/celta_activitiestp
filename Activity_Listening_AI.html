📄 Content summary — teacher reference / post-watching

The big idea: computers are brilliant at specific, narrow tasks, but they still lack the general common sense and flexible thinking that humans have.

Key points from the video:

1. Machine thinking is now part of everyday life — in our homes, offices, schools and hospitals.

2. Computer algorithms help drive cars and help doctors diagnose illnesses.

3. The term "artificial intelligence" was first used back in the 1950s.

4. AI is very good at one specific job at a time, but it can't match the broad, everyday intelligence of a person.

Useful vocabulary: algorithm · artificial intelligence · autonomous · to diagnose · common sense.
