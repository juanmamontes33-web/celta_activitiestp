Hedging & Modality 🪶
How a C1 speaker sounds careful rather than certain — softening claims, and speculating about the present and the past.

LEAD-IN
Why soften?
Which sounds more C1? 🤔
Over-strong
"Video games are bad for children. This proves it."
Hedged — C1
"Video games may have a negative effect on some children. This suggests a link."
💡 Absolute claims are easy to attack. Qualified claims sound considered — and score higher.

MODALITY
Deduction · present
Speculating about now 🔍
must be — I'm sure it's true. "He's not answering — he must be busy."
might / may / could be — it's possible. "She might be at lunch."
can't be — I'm sure it's false. "That can't be right."
English from Glory · C1 Core · Hedging & Modality04 / 10
MODALITY
Deduction · past
modal + have + past participle 🕵️
must have — sure it happened. "The ground's wet — it must have rained."
might / may / could have — possibly happened. "She may have forgotten."
can't / couldn't have — sure it didn't. "He can't have finished already."
⚠️ Spanish L1: not "must to have" — just must have + p.p.

Fix these 🚫

Soften with modals, seem/tend/suggest, adverbs and fixed phrases.
