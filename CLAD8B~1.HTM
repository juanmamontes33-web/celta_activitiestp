MFP
Target language · Meaning · Form · Pronunciation
Get it right — M·F·P
"I'm responsible for + noun/-ing" (responsibilities)
MEANING = it's my job to do this · FORM responsible for + noun/-ing (NOT of) · PRON weak for /fə/
"I managed to increase sales by 20%." (achievement)
MEANING = I succeeded in doing it · FORM managed to + base verb; past + number · PRON stress the number
"I've worked as a nurse for five years." (experience)
MEANING = started before, still true · FORM present perfect + for/since · PRON -ed: worked /t/, managed /d/, started /ɪd/
