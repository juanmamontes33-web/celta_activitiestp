📬 Two models, one toolkit

A short message to a teacher and a professional email — notice the shared phrases.
To a teacher

Dear Ms Patel,

I hope you're well. I'm writing to ask if I could hand in my essay on Friday instead of Wednesday, as I've been unwell this week. Please let me know if that's okay.

Thank you very much,
Diego

Professional

Dear Mr Lopez,

I'm writing to request the figures for the Q3 report. I was wondering if you could send them by Thursday. I've attached last quarter's version for reference.

Thank you for your help. I look forward to hearing from you.
Best regards,
Ana Ruiz

Target language

Email chunks

Functional email phrases, with MFP: Meaning · Form · Pronunciation.

📨 Opening & purpose

Dear Mr / Ms …, · Hi …,
I hope you're well.
I'm writing to …
M greet + say why you're writing.  F I'm writing to + base verb; Dear + title + surname = formal.  P weak to /tə/.

🙏 Polite requests

Could you please …?
I was wondering if you could …
Would it be possible to …?
M ask politely.  F Could/Would + base verb; I was wondering if you could + base.  P soft, rising tone; stress the request.

📎 Attachments & referring

Please find attached …
I've attached …
As mentioned, …
M point to a file / an earlier point.  F find attached + noun; I've attached = present perfect.  P at·TACHED /əˈtætʃt/.

🔁 Follow-ups & closing

I'm just following up on …
I look forward to hearing from you.
Best regards, / Kind regards,
M chase a reply / sign off.  F look forward to + -ing (gerund!); following up on + noun.  P "look forward to HEARing".
