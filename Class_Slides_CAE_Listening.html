Listening Parts 1–4 🎧
Four parts, thirty questions — and, unlike IELTS, you hear every recording twice.

OVERVIEW
The four parts
What each part asks 🗺️
Part 1 — three short extracts → multiple choice.
Part 2 — a monologue → sentence completion (the words you hear).
Part 3 — an interview/discussion → multiple choice.
Part 4 — five short monologues → multiple matching (two tasks).
English from Glory · CAE · Listening02 / 10
ADVANTAGE
You hear it twice
Use each listen differently 🔁
First listen — get the gist and pencil in likely answers.
Second listen — confirm, and fill any gaps you missed.
Don't freeze on a hard question — you get another chance.

PART 2
Sentence completion
Write the words you hear ✍️
The answer is usually the exact word(s) spoken — don't paraphrase it yourself.
Predict the part of speech from the sentence before you listen.
Check your answer makes grammatical sense in the gap.
English from Glory · CAE · Listening04 / 10
PART 4
Multiple matching
Five speakers, two tasks 🔎
Five people speak on one theme; you match each to an option (e.g. their reason and their feeling).
Read all options first, then listen for the paraphrase.
Each speaker is matched independently in both tasks.
English from Glory · CAE · Listening05 / 10
THE TRAP
Beware distractors
Sounds right… then corrected 🪤
Speakers mention an idea only to reject or qualify it: "I thought it was the price — but actually it was the service."
Listen to the whole idea before you commit, and match meaning, not a single word.
