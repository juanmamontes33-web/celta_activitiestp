📄 Model essay — structure reference (160 words)
IntroductionIt is often argued that modern technology is making people less sociable. While this view has some truth, I believe the reality is more balanced. First pointOn the one hand, it is true that people now spend hours on their phones instead of talking face to face. Furthermore, family meals are often interrupted by notifications, which can weaken real relationships. Second pointOn the other hand, technology also connects us. Thanks to video calls and social media, we can stay in touch with friends abroad and meet people who share our interests. In my view, this is a clear social benefit. ConclusionTo sum up, although technology can reduce face-to-face contact, it also creates new ways to connect. Overall, I would argue that it depends on how, not how much, we use it.
⛰️ C1 Stretch — beyond the essay

C1 Advanced keeps the compulsory essay but adds other genres in Part 2: a proposal (recommendations for a decision-maker, with headings), a report (factual, neutral, headed sections) and a review (evaluate + recommend). The register climbs: nominalisation ("the implementation of…"), hedging ("this would arguably…") and more precise linking. Try turning the essay above into a short proposal with two headed recommendations.
