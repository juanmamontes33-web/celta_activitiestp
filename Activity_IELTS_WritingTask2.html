📄 Model paragraph — reveal
Question: Some people think children should start school at a very early age. To what extent do you agree?

Introduction: It is sometimes argued that formal education should begin in early childhood. While there are clear benefits to an early start, I would argue that the drawbacks, in terms of children's wellbeing, are more significant.

Body 1 (one side): On the one hand, starting school early may help children develop social skills. For instance, young learners who interact with peers tend to adapt more quickly to group settings.

Body 2 (your view): However, a very early start can place undue pressure on children. As a result, some may lose motivation before they have even reached secondary level.

Conclusion: In conclusion, although an early start has some advantages, I believe the potential harm to children's development outweighs them.
🎓 Band booster

Band 7+ writers answer the exact question type (agree/disagree vs discuss both views), paraphrase the prompt rather than copy it, and support every claim with a reason + example. Pair this with the Hedging & Modality lesson — "This may be because…" scores higher than "This is because…".
