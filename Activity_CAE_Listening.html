🎧 Transcript — a talk on urban beekeeping
Good evening. Tonight I'll be talking about the revival of urban beekeeping. Keeping bees in cities was once rare, but over the past decade the number of city hives has roughly tripled. Many people assume honey is the main reason, yet most urban beekeepers say their real motivation is supporting biodiversity. Surprisingly, city honey is often purer than rural honey, because urban gardens contain fewer pesticides. The biggest challenge, beekeepers report, is not the bees themselves but a lack of suitable rooftop space. To get started, experts recommend taking a weekend training course before buying any equipment.

🎓 Exam strategy

Use both listens: get the gist and likely answers on the first, confirm and fill gaps on the second. Part 2: the answer is usually the exact word(s) spoken — don't paraphrase it yourself. Part 4: watch for distractors — a speaker may mention an idea only to reject it. Read all the options before the audio.
