📄 Transcript summary — teacher reference / post-listening

Neil & Georgina: The programme is about rapport — a good connection and understanding between people — and how we get on with others, socially and at work.

Quiz: In 1936, Dale Carnegie published a hugely successful self-help book on building rapport. Its title: How to Win Friends and Influence People (over 30 million copies sold).

Expert — Emily Alison (a psychologist, author of Rapport: The Four Ways to Read People): building rapport is like walking a tightrope — you must stay objective (judge by facts, not feelings) and compassionate without becoming collusive.

Four communication styles (named after animals): Monkey = friendly & cooperative, the best at building rapport; Lion = bossy, wants control; Mouse = passive; T-Rex = conflict & argument — can be frank (open and honest) but also attacking or sarcastic.
