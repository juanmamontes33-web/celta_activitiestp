C1 CORE · HEDGING & MODALITY
Sounding careful, academic and precise
Hedging softens a claim so it sounds considered, not arrogant. Modality lets you speculate and deduce — must have, might have, can't have.
🎯 At C1, overstating ("X is always true", "this proves…") costs marks. Cautious, qualified language is what distinguishes a C1 writer and speaker.
