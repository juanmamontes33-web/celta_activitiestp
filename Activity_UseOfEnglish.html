Part 1 — Multiple-choice cloze 🎯
Choose the word that fits — often a collocation, phrasal verb or linker. A, B, C or D.
Part 2 — Open cloze 🔑
Type ONE word in each gap — usually grammar words (articles, prepositions, auxiliaries, relatives).
Part 3 — Word formation 🔨
Change the ROOT word in CAPS into the right form (noun, adjective, adverb…).
Part 4 — Key word transformations ⭐
Rewrite the second sentence so it means the same, using the KEY word. Use 2–5 words; don't change the key word.
⛰️ C1 Stretch — toward C1 Advanced

At C1 Advanced the same four parts appear but harder: collocations are subtler, transformations run to 3–6 words and often combine two structures at once (e.g. inversion + a fixed phrase: "No sooner had he arrived than…"). Re-attempt any Part 4 item above and add a second grammar point to your answer.
