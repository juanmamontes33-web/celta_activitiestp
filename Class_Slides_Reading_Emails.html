MODELS
Two models · one toolkit
Teacher note vs work email 📬
To a teacher
Dear Ms Patel,
I hope you're well. I'm writing to ask if I could hand in my essay on Friday instead of Wednesday, as I've been unwell. Please let me know if that's okay.
Thank you very much,
Diego
Professional
Dear Mr Lopez,
I'm writing to request the Q3 figures. I was wondering if you could send them by Thursday. I've attached last quarter's version.
Thank you. I look forward to hearing from you.
Best regards, Ana
👉 Same toolkit — just slightly warmer to a teacher, more neutral at work.

TARGET LANGUAGE
Target language · email chunks
The email toolkit ✉️
📨 Opening & purpose
Dear Mr/Ms…, · Hi…, · I hope you're well. · I'm writing to…
🙏 Polite requests
Could you please…? · I was wondering if you could… · Would it be possible to…?
📎 Attachments
Please find attached… · I've attached… · As mentioned,…
🔁 Follow-up & closing
I'm just following up on… · I look forward to hearing from you. · Best regards,
👉 Watch out: I'm writing to + verb but look forward to + -ing!
Target language · present & drill08 / 11
MFP
Target language · Meaning · Form · Pronunciation
Get it right — M·F·P
"I'm writing to ask about…" (purpose)
MEANING = why you're emailing · FORM I'm writing to + base verb (NOT writing for ask) · PRON weak to /tə/
"I look forward to hearing from you." (closing)
MEANING = a polite ending · FORM look forward to + -ing (here to is a preposition!) · PRON "look forward to HEARing"
"I was wondering if you could send it." (polite request)
MEANING = a very polite request · FORM I was wondering if you could + base verb · PRON soft, falling; could /kəd/
