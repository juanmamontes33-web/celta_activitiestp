📝 Key facts — teacher reference

Speaker: Steve Jobs, co-founder of Apple & Pixar (Stanford Commencement, June 2005, ~15 min; watched 120M+ times).
Structure: three short stories — "connecting the dots", "love and loss", and "death".
Story 1: he dropped out of college, then dropped in on a calligraphy class — years later that shaped the Mac's beautiful fonts. "You can't connect the dots looking forward; you can only connect them looking backward."
Story 2: he was fired from Apple, the company he co-founded — but it freed him to start again. "You've got to find what you love."
Story 3: facing illness taught him: "Your time is limited, so don't waste it living someone else's life."
Closing: "Stay hungry. Stay foolish."

Target language

Talking about the future

Future forms for dreams, jobs & plans, with MFP: Meaning · Form · Pronunciation.

💭 Dreams & ambitions

I'd like to …
I hope to …
My dream is to …
I've always wanted to …
M a wish / hope.  F 'd like / hope + to + base verb.  P I'd /aɪd/; weak to /tə/.

📋 Plans & intentions

I'm going to …
I'm planning to …
I intend to …
M a decision already made.  F be going to + base verb.  P going to → "gonna".

🔮 Predictions & goals

I think I'll …
I'll probably …
In five years, I'll …
M a prediction.  F will ('ll) + base verb.  P 'll /l/.

🎯 Seeing your future

I see myself + -ing …
I aim to …
I'm working towards …
M your vision.  F see myself + -ing (gerund!); aim to + base.  P stress the goal.
