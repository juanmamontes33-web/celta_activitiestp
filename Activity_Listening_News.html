📄 Content summary — teacher reference / post-listening

The five ways to spot fake news:

1. Check the web address. Unusual or unfamiliar domain endings can suggest a site isn't a legitimate news source.

2. Examine the layout. Grammar mistakes, wrong dates, bold claims without sources and sensational images are red flags.

3. Investigate the site. Find out who wrote the article and who runs or funds the site. Hidden information is suspicious.

4. Cross-check the story. Use fact-checking websites and see whether other credible news outlets report the same thing.

5. Run a reverse image search. If the same photo appears in unrelated stories, the content may be misleading.

Key idea: building media literacy — the ability to analyse and evaluate the news.
