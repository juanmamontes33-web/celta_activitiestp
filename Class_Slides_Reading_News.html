VOCABULARY
Key words · learn before you read
NOUN PHRASE
fake news
false stories made to look like real news.
CCQ: Real or invented? Invented · Looks real? Yes
ADJECTIVE
reliable
that you can trust; usually correct and honest.
CCQ: Can you trust it? Yes · Often wrong? No
VERB
to cross-check
to confirm something by comparing other sources.
CCQ: One source or several? Several · Why? To be sure
