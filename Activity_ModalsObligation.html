MODALS OF OBLIGATION & ADVICE
Obligation · must / have to — Advice · should / ought to / had better
You must wear a seatbelt. · You should rest. · You'd better hurry!
⚠️ The big one — prohibition vs no obligation
mustn't = don't do it (forbidden) ≠ don't have to = it isn't necessary
👉 No to after must / should / had better: "must wear", "should rest", "had better go".

⛰️ C1 Stretch — subtler obligation

C1 adds nuance: be supposed to (expectation, often broken) — "You're supposed to book in advance"; be to (formal rules/orders) — "Visitors are to sign in"; and past criticism/regret with should have + V3 — "You should have told me." Also: needn't have done (you did it, but it wasn't necessary) vs didn't need to do.
