Just One More Episode

Streaming & TV

1Not long ago, we waited a whole week for the next episode of our favourite show. Today, entire seasons appear at once, and millions of us "binge-watch" — we sit down for one episode and, somehow, five hours later, we are still on the sofa. Why can't we stop?

2Part of the answer is design. Streaming platforms are built to keep us watching. The next episode starts automatically after just a few seconds, before we have time to decide. And writers end almost every episode with a cliffhanger — an exciting, unfinished moment that makes us desperate to know what happens next.

3But it isn't only clever technology. A gripping story gives our brains a small reward, a little like a good song we play again and again. We care about the characters, we want them to succeed, and stopping feels almost rude — as if we were leaving a friend in the middle of a conversation.

4There is a social side too. When everyone is talking about the same series, no one wants to be left behind, or to have the ending spoiled. "No spoilers!" has become one of the most common phrases of the streaming age.

5None of this is bad in itself. Stories have always brought people together, from theatres to living rooms. The trick, as with most pleasures, is balance: enjoy the show, but remember that the real world does not have a "skip intro" button.
