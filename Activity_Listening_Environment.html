📄 Transcript — teacher reference / read aloud

Leo (host): Welcome to Green Steps, the show about everyday people helping the planet. Today Maria tells us how she started a plastic-free project at her school. Maria, how did it begin?

Maria: Well, last year I watched a documentary about plastic in the ocean. I saw a turtle trapped in a plastic bag, and I couldn't stop thinking about it. I realised our school threw away hundreds of plastic bottles every day.

Leo: So what did you do?

Maria: I started small. I asked the head teacher if we could put recycling bins in every classroom. Then a few friends and I gave a presentation about single-use plastic in assembly. Some students laughed at first, but slowly people started bringing reusable bottles.

Leo: Did it make a difference?

Maria: More than I expected! In six months, we cut our plastic waste by about seventy percent. The school even installed a water fountain so people could refill their bottles for free.

Leo: That's amazing. Was it difficult?

Maria: Honestly, yes. Changing people's habits is hard, and some teachers said it was a waste of time. But every time I felt like giving up, I remembered that turtle.

Leo: What would you say to other young people?

Maria: Don't wait for someone else to act. You don't have to be perfect — you just have to start. One person can begin something big.

Leo: Beautifully said. Maria, thank you.
